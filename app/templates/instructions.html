1. How Store Teams Use the System Daily

This dashboard was built for store teams, not data analysts. You don't need to calculate formulas or interpret complex statistics. Use this 3-step routine:

1

Check Your Card

Find your store on the Home Grid. Look at your color badge (Action, Watch, or Optimal).

2

Read the Alert

Every card shows a direct, plain-English action message (e.g. "Waste is 2.4× target" or "Short -1.8 baristas during 11am–2pm rush").

3

Click for Deep Dive

Click your store to see the exact P&L breakdown, the lunchtime staffing gap heatmap, and the 12-month waste trend.

2. Understanding Red / Amber / Green (RAG) Badges

Every location receives an automatic operational health status based on real floor performance:

Action (Red)

Immediate Operational Attention Required

Triggered when food waste exceeds {{ settings.waste_benchmark_pct }}%, profit margin falls below {{ settings.margin_floor_pct }}%, rent & rates devour >{{ settings.occupancy_ceiling_pct }}% of sales, or peak lunchtime has a severe staff deficit (≥ 1.5 baristas short).

Watch (Amber)

Approaching Limits / Minor Deficit

Triggered when performance is close to alert thresholds, margin has dropped compared to last month, or lunch rush is slightly understaffed (between 0.5 and 1.4 baristas short).

Optimal (Green)

Healthy Operations

Profitable store with low waste, healthy margin (≥ {{ settings.margin_floor_pct }}%), and balanced counter barista staffing during peak rushes.

3. Metric Definitions & Targets

Hover over any metric name anywhere in the web app to see its pop-up explanation. Here is the complete operational guide:

Metric What it Measures Chain Target What Store Staff Can Do
Store Margin Operating profit after all food, labour, rent, and store costs, divided by net revenue. ≥ {{ settings.margin_floor_pct }}% Protect product portioning, upsell high-margin baked goods, control unnecessary waste.
Waste % Cost of discarded food and expired baked goods divided by total food sales. ≤ {{ settings.waste_benchmark_pct }}% Log all end-of-day discards accurately; adjust bake-off batch sizes according to morning footfall.
Occupancy % Monthly Rent + Business Rates divided by monthly sales. High % indicates a rent trap. ≤ {{ settings.occupancy_ceiling_pct }}% Drive volume through grab-and-go speed and app pickup to grow revenue against the fixed rent base.
Delivery Share Portion of total monthly revenue fulfilled through Dashly and RushEats apps. 12% – 18% Ensure delivery packaging and bag sealing are prepped ahead so counter service isn't slowed down.
Peak Staff Gap Difference between active baristas and required baristas during 11:00–14:00 lunchtime rush. 0 (Balanced) Shift rotas so prep/cleaning shifts happen before 11am or after 2pm; have all hands on customer service.

4. How to Refresh Data Each Month (No Developer Needed)

When new monthly sales, rota, or waste files are exported from your till or scheduling software:

  1. Go to the Upload & AI tab in the navigation bar.
  2. Select the data category (e.g. Monthly Store P&L, Itemized Food Waste, or Staff Rota).
  3. Choose your CSV file and click Cleanse & Analyze with Gemini.
  4. The system will automatically deduplicate records, normalize dates to YYYY-MM-DD, calculate new margins, and invoke the Gemini AI to write fresh plain-language operational summaries.

5. Updating Operational Thresholds

Operations managers can change company benchmarks on the Settings page without editing code. Changes take effect across all store cards and the Scoreboard immediately.
