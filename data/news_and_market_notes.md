# News and market notes

Clippings and notes collected by the Grind & Crumb marketing team. All brands and publications are fictional.

---

**Northern Business Wire, 14 January 2026** *Bean Social scraps plant milk charge* Rival chain Bean Social has dropped its 45p charge on oat, soy and almond milk across all 40 of its northern sites. "Customers told us it felt like a penalty for their dietary choices," said its marketing director.

**Yorkshire Retail Monitor, March 2026** *Coffee bean prices stay high* Wholesale arabica prices remain well above their five-year average. Several independents have raised drink prices by 10 to 20p. Analysts expect costs to ease in late 2026\.&nbsp;

**Leeds Property Journal, February 2026** *Wellington Place adds 2,000 office workers* A new office building at Wellington Place opens in autumn 2026\. Two national coffee chains are already said to be in talks for ground floor units.

**Sheffield Star Weekly, April 2026** *Kelham Island: Sheffield's fastest-growing neighbourhood* New flats, independent bars and weekend markets have made Kelham Island a destination. Only a handful of daytime cafés serve the area.

**Hull Daily Bulletin, May 2026** *City centre footfall still below pre-pandemic levels* Footfall in Hull city centre is around 8% lower than in 2019\. Shopping centres say weekdays are improving but weekends remain weak.

**Manchester Evening Post, June 2026** *Piccadilly station passenger numbers hit record* Passenger numbers at Manchester Piccadilly reached a record this spring. Several concourse food outlets report long lunchtime queues.

**UK Government notice, November 2025** *National Living Wage to rise from 1 April 2026* The National Living Wage for workers aged 21 and over will rise by about 6.7% from April 2026\.

**Café Trade Review, May 2026** *Delivery growth slows, margins squeezed* Delivery now makes up about 15% of sales for an average UK café chain. Many operators say small delivery orders lose money once commission, packaging and staff time are counted.
