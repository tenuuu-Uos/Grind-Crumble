# Stakeholder interview notes

Notes taken by the Grind & Crumb strategy intern, May-June 2026\. Lightly edited, not verbatim.

---

## Anna Whitfield, CEO

- "We've grown every year since 2017\. Sales are up again but the profit line doesn't move. I can't tell if it's one or two stores dragging us down or something across the whole chain."  
- Very proud of Leeds Trinity. "It's ramping well, it'll be one of our best within two years."  
- Wants to keep opening. Has looked at Kelham Island in Sheffield and Wellington Place in Leeds. "Leeds has been good to us, so another one there feels safe."  
- On delivery: "That's the future. Every month it's a bigger share. I'd like it at 30% of sales."  
- On the oat milk charge: finance proposed it in December to recover the extra cost of oat milk, roughly £40k a year. "A few people moaned on Google but that always happens."  
- *Board meets monthly. Gets a sales report by store each Monday, but nothing on profit until month end. "By the time I see a problem it's six weeks old."*

## Ollie Brent, Store Manager, Manchester Piccadilly

- "We're the biggest store in the chain, so we're obviously the most profitable. The rent is head office's problem, I don't see it."  
- Lunch is the hardest part. "Commuters and office people come through between 12 and 1\. If the queue is more than five deep, they walk to the station kiosks."  
- Staff turnover is "relentless". "I train someone for a month and they leave for the chain on the concourse that pays 50p an hour more."  
- Has asked for two extra lunch shifts a day. "Head office says labour is already our biggest controllable cost, so no."  
- Delivery tablets "ping constantly" at lunch. "Half of them are one americano. We drop what we're doing to bag it up."

## Jordan, Barista, Manchester Piccadilly (18 months in role)

- "There are usually three of us on at lunch when there should be five. People see the queue and leave."  
- "The delivery orders jam everything up. A rider comes for a single flat white and we've got twelve people waiting at the till."  
- "Since the new app came out people come to the counter angry because their order never showed up or their stamps disappeared."  
- Thinking about leaving. "I like the team, but every lunch is a firefight."

## Grace Okafor, Store Manager, Leeds Briggate

- "The city centre is just quieter this year. Footfall on Briggate feels down."  
- "A few regulars have said they go to the new one now because it's nearer their office. But that's a handful."  
- Pastry and lunch orders are "set by the central par levels, same as last year". She hasn't changed them. "We bin more than we used to, to be honest."  
- Seating is tight at weekends.

## Marcus Dyer, Store Manager, Hull Paragon

- "We always run out, so I order extra to be safe. I'd rather bin it than disappoint someone."  
- Cleaner left in October and hasn't been replaced. "We all chip in but it's not the same."  
- The dishwasher has broken three times this year and the boiler is old. "Engineers are always in."  
- "Hull isn't Leeds. People here are more price-sensitive and the high street is struggling."

## Sam Kowalski, Store Manager, Manchester Spinningfields

- "Lunch is everything here. It's offices. Weekends are dead and we could close."  
- "People have 30 minutes. If they can't get served in five, they go elsewhere. I can see them turn round at the door."  
- Has asked for more lunch staff. "I'm told to flex hours from mornings, but mornings are busy too."  
- Lost four baristas in the spring. "They're tired."  
- Often sells out of sandwiches by 1:15pm.

## Rohan Mehta, Head of Digital

- App v3.0 launched 2 March 2026\. "Biggest upgrade we've ever done. New look, new rewards, new menu."  
- "App downloads are up 20% since launch. That's the number I'm watching."  
- On complaints: "There was a bug with stamp migration for some accounts and a checkout freeze on older phones. v3.1 on 13 April fixed most of it. Customers just need time to adjust."  
- Doesn't track click & collect orders per week himself. "Ops owns that."  
- Wants budget for a v4 with personalised offers.
